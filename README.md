# About FSD 24

**FSD 24** is an advanced AI-powered autonomous driving system project designed to bring intelligent driving, predictive road awareness, adaptive vehicle control, and next-generation driving technology into one unified platform.

Built around a camera-based perception architecture, FSD 24 focuses on understanding road environments, predicting traffic behavior, planning driving maneuvers, and delivering smoother, more consistent vehicle control.

## Key Features

- **AI Driving Intelligence** — Advanced scene understanding and predictive driving decisions.
- **Camera-Based Perception** — Interprets road markings, traffic signals, vehicles, pedestrians, and surrounding road conditions.
- **Predictive Awareness** — Anticipates developing traffic situations and potential hazards.
- **Adaptive Speed Control** — Adjusts driving behavior according to road geometry and traffic conditions.
- **Intelligent Navigation** — Coordinates route planning with lane selection, intersections, and upcoming maneuvers.
- **Full-Comfort Suspension** — Supports predictive suspension control on compatible adaptive-suspension hardware.
- **FDS (Full Self-Drifting System)** — A dedicated closed-course driving feature designed for compatible vehicles and controlled environments.
- **Matrix Adaptive Headlights** — Supports intelligent illumination adjustments based on road geometry and surrounding traffic.
- **Parking Intelligence** — Improves parking-space recognition and low-speed maneuver planning.
- **Continuous Software Development** — Each update introduces new features, refinements, reliability improvements, and expanded system capabilities.

## Development Roadmap

FSD 24 is progressing through the 24.9.x release series, with **version 24.9.8** introducing updates across driving intelligence, suspension comfort, FDS, environmental awareness, navigation, and vehicle control.

The longer-term roadmap targets **FSD V25**, with the goal of pursuing fully autonomous driving within a defined and validated operating domain.

## System Philosophy

FSD 24 is built around four core principles:

1. **Awareness** — Understand the surrounding driving environment.
2. **Prediction** — Anticipate how road situations may develop.
3. **Precision** — Plan smooth, consistent vehicle movements.
4. **Adaptability** — Respond intelligently to changing road and traffic conditions.

## Compatibility

FSD 24 is designed as a universal camera-based driving-system project for supported vehicles, subject to compatible cameras, computing hardware, calibration, vehicle integration, and safety validation.

Not every feature is compatible with every vehicle. Suspension control and other hardware-dependent functions require the appropriate equipment.

## Safety

FSD 24's driving-assistance functionality requires active driver supervision. The driver must remain attentive and ready to take control whenever necessary. The FSD V25 full-autonomy objective is a development target, not a declaration of current unsupervised-driving capability.
